## Edit and enhance photos

### All-in-one browser editor

When you need to crop, adjust, add text and export in one place, a [free online photo editor](https://pixoate.com/photo-editor) that runs in the browser saves installing anything. This one combines 117+ adjustment settings, 100+ live effects, AI background removal, 2x or 4x upscaling, curved and gradient text, and a library of icons, shapes and animated elements on a single canvas.

**Exports:** PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, PDF, SVG, MP4 and GIF. That means one design can serve the web, print and short animated social posts.

**Typical uses:** YouTube thumbnails, product graphics, flyers, social media kits.

**How to use it**
1. Open the editor and drop in an image, or start a blank canvas (16 to 8,000 px).
2. Crop, resize or rotate, then fine-tune exposure, color and detail.
3. Add text, shapes or effects.
4. Export in the format your platform expects.

### One-click photo enhancement

<img src="https://pixoate.com/programmatic/proofs/v2/photo-enhancer/og.jpg?v=eca7b85e7458" alt="Before and after of an AI photo enhancer fixing blur, noise and low light" width="480">

Most "bad" photos have the same four problems: noise, soft edges, poor exposure and low resolution. An [AI photo enhancer](https://pixoate.com/photo-enhancer) fixes all four in one pass, with denoising, edge-aware sharpening, automatic brightness, contrast and color, and a resolution boost. A strength slider (default 0.7) keeps results from looking over-processed.

**Good for:** low-light phone shots, product listing photos, real estate images, scanned prints, screenshots.

**Free tier:** web-size downloads. Original resolution is paid.

### Filters and photo effects

If a photo is technically fine but flat, [free photo effects and filters](https://pixoate.com/photo-effects) can restyle it with cinematic, vintage, glitch or beauty looks across 100+ categories. You can compare against the original before applying, and download batch results as a ZIP.

## Restore and colorize old photos

<img src="https://pixoate.com/programmatic/proofs/v2/colorize-photo/after.webp?v=cfbc2ee7ede7" alt="Black and white family photo colorized with AI" width="480">

Restoration works best in two steps:

1. **Clean the scan first.** Run grainy or faded prints through the [photo enhancer](https://pixoate.com/photo-enhancer) to reduce noise and recover contrast.
2. **Then add color.** An [AI tool to colorize black and white photos](https://pixoate.com/colorize-photo) predicts natural skin, sky and fabric tones without manual masking. It offers 3 engines, a vividness slider for subtler or bolder color, and a 4x resolution boost for printing.

**Tips**
- Scan at 600 DPI or higher. The AI has more to work with.
- If skin tones look off, switch engines before lowering vividness.
- Keep the original black-and-white file. Colorization is an interpretation, not a record.

**Open-source option:** [DeOldify](https://github.com/jantic/DeOldify) (Python, runs locally).

## Resize and compress images

### Hit an exact file size

Visa portals, job applications, exam registrations and government forms often reject uploads over a strict limit, such as 50 KB, 100 KB or 200 KB. Lowering quality by trial and error wastes time. A tool to [compress an image to an exact KB size](https://pixoate.com/compress-image) takes the target directly and outputs JPEG, WebP, PNG or GIF, with a live preview so you can check faces and text stay readable.

**Also useful for:** faster WordPress and Shopify pages, better Core Web Vitals scores, email attachments and WhatsApp.

**Rule of thumb:** convert to WebP if the destination accepts it. You usually get the same visual quality in a noticeably smaller file.

### Enlarge small images

<img src="https://pixoate.com/programmatic/proofs/v2/upscale/og.jpg?v=8eb59ace2d92" alt="Low resolution image upscaled 4x with AI" width="480">

Classic resizing stretches pixels and blurs edges. An [AI image upscaler](https://pixoate.com/upscale) rebuilds plausible detail instead. Use 2x for a crisp, conservative result; [upscale images to 4x](https://pixoate.com/upscale) when you need large prints or the source is very small. Processing starts automatically after upload.

**Free tier:** 2x, up to an 800 px output. 3x, 4x and batch are paid.

**Open-source option:** [Upscayl](https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl) (desktop app; needs a GPU that supports Vulkan).

## Count objects and people from photos

Counting by eye is slow and unreliable past a few dozen items. Photo-based counters detect each item, number it on the image, and let you correct mistakes before trusting the total. That last step matters more than the raw accuracy claim.

### Count people in a photo

<img src="https://pixoate.com/hero-art/counting-photos/count-people.webp" alt="AI crowd counter marking each person in an event photo" width="480">

To [count people from photos](https://pixoate.com/count-people) of events, classrooms, queues or venues, upload the image and every detected person gets a numbered box. The tool switches automatically between counting individuals (small groups) and estimating density (dense crowds), so the same workflow covers a team photo and a packed concert.

**Settings that improve accuracy**
- **Sensitivity** (0 to 1, default 0.5): raise it if people are missed; lower it if objects are being counted as people.
- **Include people facing away:** turn this on for audiences photographed from behind.
- **Max accuracy mode:** cross-checks several detection engines for an [AI crowd counter](https://pixoate.com/count-people) result you can defend in a report.

**Use cases:** event attendance reporting, fire-safety occupancy checks, classroom headcounts, queue length estimates.

### Count objects in an image

<img src="https://pixoate.com/programmatic/proofs/v2/count-objects/og.jpg?v=f7b8b4264621" alt="AI object counter numbering coins and parts in a photo" width="480">

For anything repeated, such as coins, pills, screws, seedlings or cans, you can [count objects in an image](https://pixoate.com/count-objects) by marking 1 to 3 examples and letting the counter find the matches.

**Steps**
1. Upload the photo and click on one to three typical items.
2. Pick a mode: **Auto**, **Similar items** (same product repeated) or **Distinct objects** (mixed items).
3. Review the numbered dots or heatmap and fix any misses.
4. Export the tally as CSV.

**Photo tips:** shoot straight down, use even light, and avoid items stacked on top of each other.

### Inventory counts from shelf photos

<img src="https://pixoate.com/hero-art/counting-photos/inventory-counter.webp" alt="Photo-based inventory counting on warehouse shelves" width="480">

Retail and warehouse teams can do [stock-takes from shelf photos](https://pixoate.com/inventory-counter): photograph shelves, crates or pallets, review the draft counts, and add each one to a running tally sheet that exports to CSV for your spreadsheet or inventory system. Markers help avoid counting the same item twice across overlapping shots.

**Use cases:** retail cycle counts, warehouse audits, pharmacy stock, spare-parts bins.

**For developers:** to train your own detector, [Ultralytics YOLO](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics) is the common open-source starting point.

## PDF editing and conversion

### Edit text inside a PDF

<img src="https://pixoate.com/hero-art/pdf-editor.webp" alt="Editing existing text inside a PDF in the browser" width="480">

Many "free PDF editors" only let you type on top of the page. To [edit PDF text online](https://pixoate.com/pdf-editor) properly, you need to click existing text and change its words, font, size and color. This editor does that, and adds find and replace, white-out and redaction, signatures, stamps, watermarks, and page insert, delete, reorder and numbering.

**Common jobs:** fixing a typo in a signed-off contract draft, updating dates or prices, filling and signing forms.

### Convert PDF to Word

To [convert PDF to editable Word](https://pixoate.com/pdf-to-word) files, pick one of two modes:

| Mode | What you get | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edit the content | Text, tables and layout rebuilt as a real DOCX | Contracts, resumes, reports you will rewrite |
| Keep the exact appearance | Each page placed as an image inside the DOCX | Scans and complex designed layouts |

The output opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs and Apple Pages.

**Open-source options:** [pdf2docx](https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/pdf2docx) (Python library) and [Stirling-PDF](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF) (self-hosted PDF toolkit).

## OCR: extract text and data from images

### Image to text

<img src="https://pixoate.com/programmatic/proofs/v2/image-to-text/og.jpg?v=24e7abf42ba3" alt="OCR extracting editable text from a photographed document" width="480">

To [extract text from an image](https://pixoate.com/image-to-text) such as a screenshot, a whiteboard, a menu or a business card, upload it, choose from 30+ languages, and review the text before copying. Handwriting works on clear scans. You can export to `.txt`, `.md`, `.docx`, `.xlsx` or `.csv`, which helps when the source is a table.

**Accuracy tips:** crop to the text, shoot flat rather than at an angle, and use good light. Blur and glare cause most OCR errors.

**Open-source options:** [Tesseract](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) and [PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR).

### Receipts to structured data

<img src="https://pixoate.com/programmatic/proofs/v2/receipt-extractor/og.jpg?v=3562a4f4dddb" alt="Receipt photo converted to JSON with vendor, total and tax" width="480">

General OCR gives you a block of text. Expense workflows need fields. A [receipt scanner that extracts data to JSON](https://pixoate.com/receipt-extractor) returns the vendor, date, total, tax, currency and line items from photos or PDFs (up to 10 pages). The JSON is ready to paste into accounting tools or a script.

**Use cases:** expense reports, freelancer tax records, travel reimbursement, household budgeting.

## FAQ

**What is the easiest way to count people in a photo?**
Upload it to a [people counter for photos](https://pixoate.com/count-people), check the numbered boxes, and adjust sensitivity if anyone is missed. For dense crowds, density estimation is more reliable than counting heads one by one.

**How do I make a blurry photo clearer for free?**
Use an [AI tool to fix blurry photos](https://pixoate.com/photo-enhancer) for mild blur and noise. If the image is also small, run an [AI upscaler](https://pixoate.com/upscale) afterwards.

**How can I reduce a photo to under 100 KB?**
Use a compressor that accepts an exact target, like this one to [reduce image size in KB](https://pixoate.com/compress-image), and choose JPEG or WebP.

**Can I edit a scanned PDF?**
Scans are images, so text editing won't work directly. Run [OCR on the image](https://pixoate.com/image-to-text) first, or convert with the "exact appearance" mode of a [PDF to Word converter](https://pixoate.com/pdf-to-word).

**Are free online tools safe for personal photos?**
Check each service's retention policy before uploading. For sensitive images, use the open-source offline tools above.
